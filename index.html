Actors Access Guide

⚠️ RULE: IF THE TITLE SAYS "BACKGROUND", "EXTRAS", OR "STAND-IN", DO NOT SUBMIT.
🎬 The Reel Builders (Target)
📺 Episodic ✅
Scripted TV shows. This is where you hunt for Co-Star (1-5 lines) or Guest Star (heavy scene) roles.
Goal: Network credits & professional tier footage.
🎥 Feature Film ✅
Full-length movies. Non-union features are a great place to book "Day Player" roles with 2-3 solid lines of dialogue.
Goal: Day player credits & quick dialogue clips.
🚀 Pilot
Presentation ✅
First episode or short proof-of-concept pitches for networks. Often lower budget, but they cast strong actors to sell the concept.
Goal: Ground floor opportunities.
🎞️ Short Film ✅
Independent short stories. Easiest place to book Lead roles that give you 5-10 minutes of heavy dialogue.
Goal: Gritty, dramatic reel footage.
🎓 Student Film ✅
University projects (USC, AFI, NYU). Often shot on cinema-quality cameras. You can easily book the "Adult" Lead role.
Goal: Cinematic reel footage.
📱 Vertical Short Form ✅
Scripted series made for apps like ReelShort. Great for booking fast, dramatic Day Player roles.
Goal: Quick dialogue and archetype building.
💻 Web Series ✅
Scripted episodic content made for the web (YouTube, Vimeo). Great places to book speaking roles and get indie credits.
Goal: Scripted dialogue and indie credits.
🛑 The Distractions (Skip)
📸 Ad Campaign ❌
Print or video brand campaigns. Same as commercials, these provide no theatrical reel footage and should be handled by your agent.
Reason: Agency overlap & no theatrical value.
💵 Commercial ❌
Let Aqua handle this! Your agency is actively submitting you for these. Self-submitting risks agency conflict.
Reason: Agency overlap & no theatrical value.
🌍 Documentary ❌
Real-world interviews or historical reenactments. Even reenactments are rarely usable for a strong acting reel.
Reason: Rarely usable for dramatic reels.
🏢 Industrial ❌
Internal corporate training videos. Often dry, highly corporate, and rarely released publicly for you to get footage.
Reason: Boring footage, hard to obtain.
🎪 Live Project ❌
Theme park performers, haunted houses, or interactive live events.
Reason: Zero on-camera footage.
🎵 Music Video ❌
Almost entirely non-speaking or pantomime acting to music. They look visually cool, but lack dialogue.
Reason: No dialogue = No reel value.
🎙️ Podcast ❌
Strictly audio-based roles. Great for a voiceover reel, but provides absolutely zero visual footage for theatrical casting.
Reason: Zero on-camera footage.
📣 Promo ❌
Promotional videos or sizzle reels for products/brands. Like commercials, these should be handled by your agent and provide no acting footage.
Reason: Agency overlap & no theatrical value.
🗣️ Reality ❌
Unscripted formats, game shows, or dating shows. Casting directors for scripted TV and Film do not care about reality credits.
Reason: Not acting. Cannot be used on a reel.
✨ Special ❌
One-off television events (like holiday specials or live events). Often unscripted reality or variety formats that provide zero dramatic footage.
Reason: Rarely scripted acting. No reel value.
🎭 Theatre ❌
Stage plays. A completely different skill set that does not translate to on-camera film/TV casting.
Reason: Zero on-camera footage.
🎮 Video Game ❌
Usually Voiceover or Face Scans. Excellent for money, but provides no usable dramatic on-camera footage.
Reason: No on-camera acting footage.
🛠️ Workshop ❌
Developmental readings or rehearsals of a project in progress. Excellent for networking with writers/directors, but provides no on-camera footage.
Reason: Zero on-camera footage.
